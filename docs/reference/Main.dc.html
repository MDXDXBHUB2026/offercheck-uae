High risk indicators

This offer shows several patterns common in recruitment scams.

Do not pay, and do not send identity documents, until you have verified the employer through an official channel. This is a set of risk indicators, not a verdict about any company.

Score = 40 (high) + 20 + 20 + 20 (medium) = 100, capped at 100. Bands: high 60+, medium 30+.

Evidence from the offer

Fee requested High +40
...pay a processing fee of AED 1,850 by transfer today only...

A genuine employer does not ask a candidate to pay to receive a job or visa. Rule RF-001.

Identity documents requested Medium +20
...Send your passport copy and Emirates ID on WhatsApp.

Documents are requested before any verified interview or contract. Rule RF-003.

Urgency pressure Medium +20
...by transfer today only.

Pressure to act immediately leaves no time to verify. Rule RF-004.

Free email contact Medium +20
Contact: hr.gulfhorizon@gmail.com

An HR contact on a free email domain, with no company domain to check. Rule RF-005.

Verify through official channels

Before paying or sharing documents, confirm the employer and the offer yourself.

  • Look the offer up through the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) official services.
  • Ask for the company trade licence number and check it with the licensing authority.
  • Meet or video-call the employer using contact details you found yourself, not the ones in the message.

What this check cannot do

  • It finds known warning patterns. A scam without these patterns can pass, and a real offer can trigger one.
  • Misspelled or unusual wording may be missed by the fixed rules.
  • It does not confirm that a company exists or that a job is real.
  • It is not legal advice and gives no verdict about any named company.
Offer text is treated as untrusted input: instructions hidden inside an offer never change the score.
