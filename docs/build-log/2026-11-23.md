## Built
- **Tokens.** Every colour in `src/app/globals.css` was already a custom property; a guard test (`src/app/themeTokens.test.ts`) now fails if a hex, rgb, hsl or named colour appears outside the token blocks. Separately, every `var(--…)` in the old and new stylesheet was resolved to its dark value and the rules compared: 485 rules, identical, except the removed `.chip-soon` rule (its only users were the two "coming soon" chips).
- **Light palette.** Warm off-white page (`#faf8f4`), white cards, dark slate text, one blue (`#1d4ed8`) for the primary action, links and the focus ring. Risk colours keep their meaning (high red, medium amber, low green) with the visible text labels unchanged. Glows and the aurora are quiet in light. Three identical copies of the light block: `:root[data-theme="light"]`, `:root[data-theme="system"]` inside `@media (prefers-color-scheme: light)`, and the print block (paper is always light, whatever the theme on screen).
- **Contrast, tested.** `src/app/themeContrast.test.ts` checks 60 pairs per theme from the tokens (55 colour pairs, text 4.5:1 and graphics 3:1, plus 5 hero text colours over the worst video pixel) and writes the table `docs/theme-contrast.md`. One real failure was fixed in the palette, not by lowering the bar: light hero text in green over a black video pixel was 4.39:1, so the light glass went from 0.90 to 0.92.
- **Mechanism.** `src/components/shell/theme.ts` (store, same pattern as the language, animation and text size stores), key `offercheck_theme_v1`, values `system`, `dark`, `light`; no saved value means the dark default. A script in the head (`THEME_INIT_SCRIPT`, `src/lib/themeInit.ts`) sets `data-theme` on `<html>` before first paint; `restoreTheme()` does the same on mount (it also covers React's development remount clearing the attribute, as the Next.js guide `preventing-flash-before-hydration.md` describes). The script carries this request's CSP nonce, read from the request's own `Content-Security-Policy` header (`nonceFromCsp`); `src/lib/csp.ts`, `src/proxy.ts` and the security headers are untouched and `'unsafe-inline'` is still absent from script-src. The browser's `theme-color` meta tags use the media-query form, and an explicit Light or Dark choice also sets them.
- **Settings.** The Theme row has System, Dark and Light working; the "coming soon" chips, the `THEME_NOTE` sentence and their Arabic entries were removed. No new user-facing string: "Theme", "System", "Dark" and "Light" already had Arabic.
- **Clear my data** also removes `offercheck_theme_v1` and returns the page to dark.

## Arabic strings, Phase 5b (marked `[review]` in `src/lib/i18nAr.ts`; none read by a native speaker)
New (the /about guide, in the order shown):
1. Five common job-scam patterns → `خمسة أنماط شائعة لاحتيال الوظائف`
2. These are the five warning signs Offer Check's fixed rules look for. One sign does not prove a scam, and finding none does not mean an offer is real. → `هذه هي علامات التحذير الخمس التي تبحث عنها قواعد Offer Check الثابتة. وجود علامة واحدة لا يثبت أنه احتيال، وعدم وجود أي علامة لا يعني أن العرض حقيقي.`
3. A fee before you join → `رسوم قبل أن تنضم`
4. You are asked to pay for a visa, training, a uniform, a medical test or "processing" before you start work. → `يُطلب منك دفع مبلغ لتأشيرة أو تدريب أو زي موحّد أو فحص طبي أو «معالجة» قبل أن تبدأ العمل.`
5. Do not pay. Confirm the offer yourself on the official sites first. → `لا تدفع. تأكد من العرض بنفسك أولًا على المواقع الرسمية.`
6. A request for card or login details → `طلب بيانات البطاقة أو بيانات الدخول`
7. You are asked for a one-time code, a password, a card number or an online banking login, for example to "set up your salary account". → `يُطلب منك رمز لمرة واحدة أو كلمة مرور أو رقم بطاقة أو بيانات الدخول إلى الخدمة المصرفية عبر الإنترنت، مثلًا «لإعداد حساب راتبك».`
8. Do not share them with anyone who contacted you first. → `لا تشاركها مع أي شخص تواصل معك أولًا.`
9. Identity documents sent early → `إرسال وثائق الهوية مبكرًا`
10. You are asked for a passport, an Emirates ID or a bank statement before you have a written offer or contract. → `يُطلب منك جواز سفر أو هوية إماراتية أو كشف حساب بنكي قبل أن يكون لديك عرض أو عقد مكتوب.`
11. Wait until you have confirmed the employer. Send only what is needed, and hide numbers you do not need to show. → `انتظر حتى تتأكد من صاحب العمل. أرسل ما يلزم فقط، وأخفِ الأرقام التي لا حاجة لإظهارها.`
12. Pressure to act today → `ضغط للتصرف اليوم`
13. The message says the offer ends tonight, or that you must reply or pay within hours. → `تقول الرسالة إن العرض ينتهي الليلة، أو إن عليك الرد أو الدفع خلال ساعات.`
14. Slow down. Take time to check, and ask someone you trust before you reply. → `تمهّل. خذ وقتك في التحقق، واسأل شخصًا تثق به قبل أن ترد.`
15. A free email sender → `مرسل ببريد إلكتروني مجاني`
16. A named company writes to you from a free address such as Gmail or Yahoo, not from the address of its own website. → `شركة معروفة بالاسم تراسلك من عنوان مجاني مثل Gmail أو Yahoo، لا من عنوان موقعها الخاص.`
17. Look up the company's own website yourself and compare the address. Do not use contact details from the message. → `ابحث بنفسك عن موقع الشركة الرسمي وقارن العنوان. لا تستخدم بيانات الاتصال الواردة في الرسالة.`
18. Check an offer in the Offer Analyzer → `افحص عرضًا في فاحص العروض`
19. Report a scam → `الإبلاغ عن احتيال`
